# Fluid's flow system

_Generated by `scripts/describe-flows.ts` from `brain/flows/*.json` (built by `npm run brain:flows`). Edit the generator, not this file._

Source: Fluid flow system v2, distilled from 31 client strategies (fluid-flow-strategist skill). One structure, two versions: **replenishable** (Used up and rebought (supplements, peptides, skin/hair care, cosmetics, CPG). Post-purchase teaches use; upsell = reorder / next step in the routine; winback when supply runs out.) and **one-off** (Lasts (apparel, jewelry, home, gear, digital). Post-purchase makes them glad they bought; upsell = complementary core product; winback = longer style-refresh / upgrade window.). Same flows, email counts and timings; only the goals differ.

**Core:** pop-up + Welcome Flow (7 Emails + 2 SMS), Site Abandonment Flow (2 Emails), Browse Abandonment Flow (3 Emails + 1 SMS), Abandoned Cart Flow (4 Emails + 1 SMS), Post-Purchase Flow (4 Emails), Winback Flow (2 Emails).  
**Optional (propose with the number that justifies it):** Sunset / Re-engagement Flow (3 Emails), Replenishment Flow (2 Emails + 1 SMS).

## Opt-in Pop-up (4 steps)

Turn site traffic into owned subscribers at the highest submit rate possible, and learn which of three product groups / goals each subscriber came for so Welcome Email #1 opens on their problem, not ours.

- **Target:** 10%+ submit rate
- **Answer stored as:** `{{popup.property}}` → welcome: conditional split on the answer → Email #1 (Option #1) / #1 v2 (Option #2) / #1 v3 (Option #3)

#### Step #1: Teaser + Collect Zero Party Data

- **Headline:** Want {{offers.welcome}}?
- **Fields / answers:** {{popup.option_1}} · {{popup.option_2}} · {{popup.option_3}}
- Headline states exactly the welcome offer the subscriber gets (the Email #5 upgrade is never promised up front)
- One question with three answers; each answer must map to real products (the three groups new customers actually start with: DATA)
- Answers are worded the way a shopper would say them
- Gets a micro-commitment before asking for the email, which lifts opt-in rate
- Creative: the hero product for the brand, nothing else competing

#### Step #2: Collect Email

- **Headline:** Where should we send it?
- **Sub:** {{offers.welcome}} off your first order, plus [what else they get: guide, early access, the breakdown people ask for]
- **Fields / answers:** Email
- **Button:** Continue
- Sub-headline says what they get
- Optional trust line under the button

#### Step #3: Collect SMS (optional for the subscriber)

- **Headline:** Want it texted to you too?
- **Sub:** Angle on what SMS gets them: restock alerts, new flavours/drops, early access. A few texts a month.
- **Fields / answers:** Phone number
- **Button:** Yes, text me
- **Secondary:** No thanks, email is fine
- **Consent:** By submitting you agree to receive recurring automated marketing texts. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel.
- Its own step, always skippable
- Consent line present

#### Step #4: Confirmation

- **Headline:** You're in.
- **Sub:** Your code is {{offers.welcome_code}} for {{offers.welcome}} off [where it works]. It's in your inbox now.
- **Button:** Shop {{popup.hero_product}}
- The code, where it works (exclusions), one button to shop

**Question styles that worked**

| Version | Style | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Replenishable | Goal | What is your primary wellness goal right now? (supplements) |
| Replenishable | Product type | What are you shopping for today? Salts / Seasoning Blends / ... (SPQR) |
| Replenishable | Preference | How do you like your jerky? (El Viejon) |
| Replenishable | Pain point | What's the hardest part of staying consistent? (Moneta) / What are you hoping LEVL helps with? Afternoon slump / Stress + recovery / Everyday hydration |
| Replenishable | Research category | What are you researching right now? (peptides) |
| One-off | Category | What are you shopping for? Classic Gold Chains / Gold Rings / Everyday Bracelets (Gold Urban) |
| One-off | Who it's for | Who are you shopping for? (LGNDRY Fit) |
| One-off | Style | What's your style preference? Classic & Timeless / Warm & Versatile / Modern & Bold (Daily Belts) |
| One-off | Occasion | What occasion are you shopping for? Birthday / Wedding or formal / Vacation (Ellaé Lisqué) |
| One-off | Fit or compatibility | What type of fence do you have? (FencePot) |
| One-off | Customer type | Individual / Business Owner / Event Planner (Decals.com) |
| One-off | Intent | Buy A Drone / Repair My Drone / Upgrade My Fleet (Drone Doctor) |

**Offer options:** Percent off: 5%–20%, 10% most common; Dollar off for high-ticket, framed as a welcome gift (Orion Moto: $25 off); Tiered/bundle savings (Daily Belts: unlock up to 30% off); No discount for expert-led or service brands (Drone Doctor: a free expert recommendation).

**Strategy note under the pop-up:** Why this question: it gets a micro-commitment and tells us which pain point / product group the subscriber walked in with; Where the answer is stored ({{popup.property}}) and that it's reused in campaigns and launches; That the headline offer is exactly what they get; the Email #5 upgrade lands as a genuine upgrade; The creative direction for step 1.

## Post-Purchase Flow (4 Emails)

Replenishable: make the first order go well, teach them to use it, collect the review, drive the second order. One-off: make them glad they bought, get the review and photo, cross-sell into the next core product.

- **Trigger:** Metric: Placed Order
- **Filter:** Placed Order equals 1 over all time (new customers)
- **Re-entry:** once only
- **Splits used:** none

<details><summary>Flow map</summary>

- **wait 5 minutes** → 📧 **email-1**: “Thank you, {{first_name}}”
- **wait 1 day** → 📧 **email-2**: “How to get the best results from your {{event.ProductName}}”
- **wait 7 days** → 📧 **email-3**: “How's it going?”
- **wait 2 days** → 📧 **email-4**: “Picked for you: what goes with your {{event.ProductName}}”

</details>

#### Email #1: Thank You + Order Confirmation

- **Send Time:** 5 minutes later
- **Email Topic:** Thank you, {{first_name}}
- **Design:** Plain text  ·  template `post-purchase/01-thank-you-plain`
- **Sending to:** New customers
- **Offer:** None
- **Goals:**
  - Plain text, from the founder
  - Thank them for trusting the brand
  - Build a personal connection
  - Set shipping expectations so nobody has to email support
  - Offer support
  - Don't duplicate the store's transactional confirmation
  - _One-off:_
    - Offer help with sizing, setup, care or styling
    - No second-purchase offer yet: reassurance and a good first impression

#### Email #2: How to use it / get the most out of it (+ second-purchase offer)

- **Send Time:** 1 day later
- **Email Topic:** How to get the best results from your {{event.ProductName}}
- **Design:** Yes  ·  template `post-purchase/02-get-ready`
- **Sending to:** New customers
- **Offer:** Second-purchase code below the teaching ({{offers.second_purchase_code}}, 10–20% / 10–25%)
- **Goals:**
  - _Replenishable:_
    - How to use it for the best result: how much, when, with what, what to expect and when (category version in industries-replenishable.md)
    - This is the email that protects the reorder: people who use it wrong don't see results
    - Below the teaching: the second-purchase code; subscription or membership first if the client has it
  - _One-off:_
    - The ownership guide for what they bought, per product type: apparel styling/try-on/care/exchanges; jewelry care/sizing/warranty/layering; home installation/placement/care; gear setup/safety/storage/maintenance; digital how-to
    - Below the guide: the second-purchase code and complementary recommendations from the pairing map (DATA)

#### Email #3: Plain text email from the founder

- **Send Time:** 7 days later
- **Email Topic:** How's it going?
- **Design:** Plain text  ·  template `post-purchase/03-check-in-plain`
- **Sending to:** New customers
- **Offer:** None
- **Goals:**
  - Plain text from the founder
  - Ask for the review at the moment they've formed an opinion
  - Invite a reply so problems get caught early
  - No discount, no product grid
  - _Replenishable:_
    - "How's it going?" as the specific question only this brand can ask ("Did you finish the can?", "Did the COA check out?")

## Offer ladder

Anchored on the client's welcome offer (W).

| Where | Default |
|---|---|
| Pop-up, Welcome #1–#4, SMS #1 | W (5–20%, most often 10%; $ off or no discount for high-ticket / expert-led one-off brands) |
| Welcome #5 + SMS #2 | W + 5 points for 24 hours (dollar offers double) |
| Site Abandonment | None (welcome code mentioned if still valid) |
| Browse #2–#3 | About W; smaller on high-ticket |
| Abandoned Cart #3–#4 | Mystery: teaser above the real value. 'Up to 30%' → real 15% (20% when W ≥ 15%) |
| Post-Purchase #2 and #4 | Second-purchase code 10–20% (one-off up to 25%) |
| Winback #1–#2 | The ceiling: 15–25% (or $50) |
| Replenishment #2 (optional) | ~15%, 72-hour expiry; none in #1 |

- Every amount is proposed for client approval
- Subscription, membership and rewards lead where the client has them; codes become the fallback
- High-ticket brands discount less, or in dollars; state exclusions (MAP, custom quotes)
- Ongoing offers sit beside codes, never stacked: 'codes can't be combined'
- Gift framing over discount framing for emotional / occasion purchases

## Flow rules

- A purchase exits Welcome, Site Abandonment, Browse Abandonment and Abandoned Cart
- One retargeting flow at a time, by intent: Cart beats Browse, Browse beats Site Abandonment
- Flow filter on Welcome and the abandonment flows: placed order 0 times since starting the flow. Smart Sending off
- Re-entry off for Welcome; on for the abandonment flows
- Non-converters from the discount flows are suppressed from further flow discounts for a period (LEVL: 60 days)
- SMS only to contacts with SMS consent
- Category rules from the industries files (peptide compliance review before every send, age gating, claims rules)
- One-off: offer exclusions stated on every email that carries a code
