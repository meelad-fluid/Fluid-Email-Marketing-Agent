# Welcome Email #1: the onboarding style-test copy

The templated welcome email every new client gets at onboarding. The bot designs it three ways (Bold & graphic, Clean & minimal, Balanced: `../../../design/fluid-email-designer/references/style-directions.md`) so the client can pick the style for their whole account. Same copy in all three, so only the look differs.

Fill every [slot] from the client's own sources only: `research/onboarding-doc.md`, `brand/brand-kit.md`, the website and memory.md. Never invent an offer, a number, a review or a claim. If the brand has no welcome offer on file, use "Welcome" framing and their best seller instead of a code, and note "offer to confirm" in NOTES. It's a real Welcome Email #1, so the usual copy rules apply (`speak-to-the-avatar.md`, `copy-rules.md`, the Fluid QC copy checklist): third-grade level, plain, about the main avatar, 150–250 words.

Save as `copy/style-options-welcome.md`:

```
# Style Options · Welcome Email #1
Status: onboarding style test (the client picks a design style from three versions of this email)

**Talking to:** [main avatar by what they do], who just signed up because [the reason from the research] when [the moment].
**Idea:** a warm, simple hello that gets them their first order.

- **Flow:** Welcome · Email #1 (sent right after signup)
- **Sender:** [proposed sender persona, e.g. "Hannah from Brand"]
- **Offer:** [welcome offer from the site/brief, e.g. 10% off with WELCOME10] or "none on file (offer to confirm)"

SUBJECT (pick): [Your 10% off is inside / Welcome to Brand, here's 10% off]
SUBJECT (option): [a second, plain option]
PREVIEW (pick): [finishes the subject in one line]

## Hero
HEADLINE: [Welcome + the one thing they get, plainly: "Welcome! Here's 10% off your first order."]
SUBHEADLINE: [one or two sentences: what the brand is in their words + the offer]
OFFER: [CODE WELCOME10 · 10% off your first order] (only if on file)
BUTTON: [Shop Now, in the brand's CTA casing] → [best page, usually the home or best sellers]

## Why [Brand] (3 short points)
- [Point 1: the main reason customers pick them, from reviews or the site]
- [Point 2]
- [Point 3]

## Start with our best sellers (3 products)
- [Product 1 name]: [one line on what it does in their hands] · [price]
- [Product 2] …
- [Product 3] …
BUTTON: [Shop Best Sellers]

## What customers say
QUOTE: "[a real review, verbatim, that answers the main doubt]"
NAME: [first name + initial, as shown on the site]

## Close
HEADLINE: [the offer again, plainly: "Your 10% off is waiting."]
BUTTON: [Shop Now] → [same link]
FOOTER: [the brand's address, unsubscribe]

NOTES: sources for every fact (site page, review, brief); anything to confirm with the client.
```
