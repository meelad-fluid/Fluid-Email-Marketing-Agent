**Email Topic:** Welcome to Gold Urban - your elevated look starts here

- **Brand mission:** Deliver the expensive look of gold jewelry at an accessible price without compromising on style.

**Design:** Yes

- **Headline:** Your cart is waiting.

- **Subheadline:** We saved your picks so you can finish your look in a few clicks.

# Post-Purchase Flow (4 Emails)

## Email #1: Thank You + Order Confirmation

**Send Time:** 5 minutes later

**Email Topic:** Thank you for your order

**Design:** Plain text

**Sending to:** New Customers

**Goals:**

- Come from the founder.

- Thank the customer for trusting Gold Urban.

- Build a personal connection by reinforcing the mission to make an elevated look accessible.

- Confirm that the order is being prepared.

- Offer support for shipping, sizing, care, warranty, or styling questions.

- Explain that the customer will receive helpful guidance before the product arrives.

- Avoid introducing a second-purchase discount yet; focus on reassurance and a positive first impression.

**Send Time:** 1 day later

**Email Topic:** Your next look starts here - 20% off your second purchase

**Design:** Yes

**Sending to:** New Customers

**Goals:**

- Provide useful product-care and styling guidance.

- Explain how to keep gold-plated jewelry looking its best.

- Introduce a second-purchase offer of 20% off with code NEXTLOOK20.

- Position the offer as a customer-only sale for building a fuller jewelry rotation.

- Promote complementary products based on the original order.

- Recommend a bracelet to pair with a chain or a Byzantine Chain to pair with a bracelet.

- Introduce Buy Any 2, Get the 3rd Free with code GETFREE as an alternative for customers building a three-piece collection.

- Clarify that NEXTLOOK20 and GETFREE cannot be combined.

**Code:** NEXTLOOK20

**Value:** 20% off the second purchase
