**Sending to:** New Customers

**Goals:**

- Send the email from the founder.

- Introduce a **15% off second-purchase sale** to increase repeat purchase rate.

- Position the second purchase as a way to secure another look for the same trip, after-party, birthday weekend, photoshoot, or upcoming event.

- Recommend accessories, complementary ready-to-ship looks, or a second outfit.

- Remind them that the piece was designed to make them feel seen, celebrated, and unforgettable.
