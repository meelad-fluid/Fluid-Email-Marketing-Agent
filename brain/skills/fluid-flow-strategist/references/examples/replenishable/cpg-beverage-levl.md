LEVL Email Flow Strategy

# Optin

## Step #1: Teaser + Collect Zero Party Data

**Headline:** Want 10% off?

**Question:** What are you hoping LEVL helps with?

- **Option #1:** Afternoon slump

- **Option #2:** Stress + recovery

- **Option #3:** Everyday hydration

**Strategy**

- Gets a micro-commitment before we ask for an email, which lifts opt-in rate

- Tells us which of the three pain points from the brand brief the subscriber walked in with, so Email #1 opens on their problem instead of ours

- All three answers map to a benefit LEVL can support with compliant language

- Answer is stored as the levl_reason profile property and reused in campaigns and future SKU launches

- The headline states 10%, which is exactly what the subscriber receives at signup. The upgrade to 15% in Email #5 is never promised up front, so it lands as a genuine upgrade rather than the delivery of something already owed

- **Creative:** Açaí + Berry can, plum against the monochrome UI, no other product shown

## Step #2: Collect Email

**Headline:** Where should we send it? **Sub:** 10% off your first order, plus the ingredient breakdown most people ask us for. **Field:** Email **Button:** Continue

## Step #3: Collect SMS

**Headline:** Want it texted to you too? **Sub:** Restocks, new flavours, and where to find LEVL near you. A few texts a month, nothing more. **Field:** Phone number **Button:** Yes, text me **Skip link:** No thanks, email is fine

## Step #4: Confirmation

**Headline:** You're in. **Sub:** Your code is LEVL10 for 10% off, and it works on any 12-pack. It's in your inbox now. **Button:** Shop Açaí + Berry **Secondary link:** Find LEVL near you
