**Email topic:** Your spice cabinet called. It misses SPQR.

**Design:** Yes

**Sending to:** First time customers who have never purchased again

**Goals:**

Re-engage customers who likely still cook at home but have not placed a second order.

Show best sellers and complementary products based on their first purchase.

Call out XL bottles.

Call out free shipping over $55.

Invite them back without immediately discounting too heavily.

Feature best sellers.
