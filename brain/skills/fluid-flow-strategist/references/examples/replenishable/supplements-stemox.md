## Site Abandonment Flow (2 Emails)

### Email #1: Gentle Reminder

**Send Time:** 2 hours after site visit (no browsing activity)

**Email Topic:** Were You Just Checking Us Out?

**Design:** Yes

**Sending To:** New subscribers who landed on the site but did not browse or add items to cart

**Goals:**

- Gently re-engage visitors who landed but didn't explore

- Remind them of the Stemox mission and brand values

- Encourage them to explore the product catalog by benefit category

- Provide a clear entry point into the shopping experience

### Email #2: Best Sellers Showcase

**Send Time:** 1 day after site visit

**Email Topic:** Take a Look at These Best Sellers

**Design:** Yes

**Sending To:** New subscribers who abandoned the site

**Goals:**

- Showcase top-performing products to reduce decision paralysis

- Highlight the clinically proven, vegan, and non-GMO benefits

- Drive traffic back to the site with specific product recommendations

- Emphasize quality and transparency

## Browse Abandonment Flow (3 Emails)

### Email #1: Product Reminder

**Send Time:** 2 hours after browsing (product viewed but not added to cart)

**Email Topic:** Still Thinking About This?

**Design:** Yes

**Sending To:** Subscribers who viewed products but didn't add to cart

**Goals:**

- Remind subscribers of the specific product they viewed

- Highlight key benefits and the science behind the product

- Emphasize 100% organic, herb-based science and bioavailability

- Reduce friction by providing a direct link back to the product

### SMS #1: Browse Abandonment Reminder

**Send Time:** Immediately after Email #2 (approximately 1 day after browsing)

**Goals:** Simply remind subscribers about the items they were viewing and the benefits of natural wellness support

**Copy:** Hey [First Name]! Still interested in [Product Name]? We're here to support your wellness journey. Shop now: [link]

### Email #2: Reminder + Social Proof + Incentive

**Send Time:** 1 day after browsing

**Email Topic:** Everyone Is Loving Stemox

**Design:** Yes

**Sending To:** Subscribers who abandoned product browsing

**Goals:**

- Include customer testimonials specifically related to the product category they viewed

- Provide social proof to overcome purchase hesitation

- Offer a small incentive to complete the purchase

- Create a sense of community and FOMO

### Email #3: Reminder + Incentive Expiry

**Send Time:** 1 day after Email #2 (approximately 2 days after browsing)

**Email Topic:** Sh… Don't Tell Anyone About This

**Design:** Yes

**Sending To:** Subscribers who haven't purchased after previous browse abandonment emails

**Goals:**

- Create urgency by letting them know their special incentive is expiring soon

- Use playful, conversational tone to stand out in inbox

- Drive immediate action through scarcity messaging

- Final push to convert window shoppers
