## 1b. What sells (Shopify, last 90 days)
_Or: "No Shopify access yet."_
- **Best sellers** (revenue, units) and **best first-order products**
- **AOV** and order-value spread → offer threshold
- **Repeat:** % of first-time buyers who reorder; median days to 2nd order
- **Paths:** first product → next product (top 5)
- **Cohorts to email** (with sizes): one-time 30–60d · one-time 60–120d · due to reorder · lapsed · VIPs · bought A never B · B2B

## 2. The brand
- **What they sell** (hero product first, prices verified on site)
- **Mission / founder story** in their words
- **Why it's different**: the mechanism, in one plain sentence
- **Proof bank**: review count and rating, certifications, guarantees, press, clinical data — each with where it lives
- **Offers and rules**: max discount, current offers, exclusions, subscription terms
- **Visual identity**: fonts, colors, photography, logo (for the designer)

## 3. Avatars
One section per real customer type (usually 2–3; often the pop-up's three answers). For each:

### Avatar <n>: <a name that says who they are, e.g. "The 52-year-old who feels herself getting weaker">
- **Who they are:** age range, life stage, what they're doing when they find the brand (sourced)
- **Problems they're facing:** what's going wrong, in their words
- **Circumstances they're in:** their current situation, day to day
- **The desire:** what they want their life to look like, in their words
- **What keeps them lying awake at night:** the fear underneath. _Not applicable for this brand_ when nothing does; then one line on what actually drives the purchase (gifting, fun, convenience).
- **What they think of themselves:** (Copywriting SOP; skip if not applicable)
- **Objections before buying:** price, trust, "does it work for me"
- **Their words:** 3–6 verbatim phrases from reviews/comments

- **Best products / entry point:** what they start with
