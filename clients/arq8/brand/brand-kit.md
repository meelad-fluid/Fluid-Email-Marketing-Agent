# Arq8 brand kit (for email design)

Pulled from arq-8.com and Arq8's Shopify product imagery on 2026-11-16 (no visual guidelines were supplied).

## Type
- **Headlines:** GT Canon Standard Light (+ Light Italic for emphasis) — Arq8's site serif. Files: Shopify CDN `GT-Canon-L-Standard-Light.woff2`, `…-Light-Italic.woff2`. Sentence case, tight leading (~1.05), slight negative tracking.
- **Body / labels:** Geist (Google Fonts) 400–700. Labels in caps with wide tracking (0.12–0.18em).

## Color
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ink / buttons / dark cards | #191919 |
| Silver studio greys (backgrounds) | #FFFFFF → #F3F3F3 → #ECECEC |
| Strawberry (accent, splashes, flavor card) | #E2404F (light #FF7A88, deep #C42E3D) |
| Mango / yellow (splashes, flavor card) | #FFB020 (light #FFE27A, deep #F28A1E) |
| Site teal (links on site; not used in email yet) | #108474 |

## Components
- **Button:** #191919 fill, 2px #000 border, drop shadow (0 10px 22px rgba(25,25,25,.28)), Geist 600 14px caps, 0.12em tracking, square corners.
- **Code tag:** dashed 1.5px #191919 border on white, "CODE" small + code bold.
- **Cards:** square corners, white on grey or #191919 for the highlighted tier.
- **Color:** every email carries strawberry/mango color — splashes behind product, floating gummies, gradient band (see memory.md preferences).

## CTA casing
**ALL CAPS.** arq-8.com writes its primary buttons in caps: "SHOP NOW", "CHECK OUT", "JOIN NOW" (home, about, 2026-11-17). Secondary text links are lower/title case on the site ("learn more", "science behind", "Shop All"); in email every button is ALL CAPS.
