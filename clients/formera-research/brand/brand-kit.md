- Style: **TBC** from site CSS (shape/radius/colour). Default: solid ink button, white text, small radius.

## Imagery
- Site: clean product renders on light gray-blue fields, a hero background image, simple line icons for purity, delivery, shipping and support (site home).
- Allowed in email (RUO): real Formera vials and packaging, real COA documents (cropped to lot/date/method rows), lab equipment (HPLC instrument, analytical balance, glassware, sample racks), shipping boxes. Never people, bodies, syringes, scales, gyms, mirrors, before/after.
- Need from client: product photography (vials with labels readable), a sample COA PDF, packaging/shipping photos.

## Trust points (use only those marked OK in compliance.md §5)
- COA library with lab report PDFs for every listed product (OK).
- Multiple dated COAs on key products (OK, as listed).
- US-only shipping, USPS, 2–6 business days standard (OK); free shipping over $200 (OK, confirm).
- Support hours and phone number (OK).
- HOLD until substantiated: 99%+ purity, HPLC + MS, "8x tested", next-business-day shipping, satisfaction guarantee, 4.8/55 reviews.

Friendly, caring, helpful (brief). In practice: plain-spoken procurement help, never hype. See research/onboarding-doc.md §4.
