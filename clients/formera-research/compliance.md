## 1. Classification (SOP §2)

**Classification: NOT YET ASSIGNED → ESCALATE.** We do not classify from the brand calling itself "research grade" (SOP §2). Reasons for escalation, each sourced:

- **Investigational GLP-class compound as the #1 seller and requested promotion.** The brief lists "GLP-3 R 20mg" as a best seller and asks for "buy one get one free on GLP-3's right now" (brief). It does not appear in the public catalog (site /shop/ pages 1–4, 55 products, checked 2026-11-20). Promoting it is the highest-risk activity in the brief (SOP §4.1 subject matter, §10). → ESCALATE; no flows or campaigns feature it or the BOGO until legal review clears it.
- **Catalog items that share names with FDA-approved drugs** (possible classification B, SOP §2): Tesamorelin 10 mg and Tesamorelin and Ipamorelin 13/3 MG, PT-141 (Bremelanotide) 10 mg, HCG 10,000 IU, HMG 75 IU, Gonadorelin 2/5/10 mg, B12 1000 mcg, Melanotan-I 5 mg (site /shop/). → ESCALATE per product.
- **Distribution signals that may suggest individual/human use** (SOP §2 D, §5): the age gate offers "Private Research" as a buyer category next to "Laboratory / Company" and "Academic Institution"; the catalog sells "Bac water 10 ml" (site home age gate; /shop/ p1). → Legal review of audience and catalog.
- The site states Formera is a chemical supplier, not a 503A/503B compounding pharmacy (site footer/home). Noted; does not by itself establish classification A.

**Do-not-feature list (any channel) until cleared:** GLP-3 R (any size), Tesamorelin, Tesamorelin and Ipamorelin, PT-141, HCG, HMG, Gonadorelin, B12, Melanotan-I, Melanotan-II, Bac water. ## 6. Product naming rules

- Use catalog names exactly as the site lists them (e.g. "BPC-157 10 mg", "NAD+ 1000mg", "GLOW", "KLOW", "SS-31 50mg").
- Blends (GLOW, KLOW) are named only; never expand them into what they are "for". Composition only if the client supplies it from the COA/spec sheet.
- Never use effect-based category names (no "recovery", "metabolic", "longevity", "skin", "GH" categories). Group by format only: single compounds, blends, sizes.
- No mechanism or pharmacology lines about any product (SOP §5: escalate, don't write).
