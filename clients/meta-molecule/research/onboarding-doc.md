### Avatar 1: The independent lab buyer who's been burned by a vendor before
- **Who they are:** buys for an independent or private lab; orders single vials and small bundles online; selected "Independent / private lab" at the gate (site gate).
- **Problems they're facing:** can't tell whether a vendor's COA belongs to the vial they'll receive; resellers relabel and disappear; support doesn't answer (brand line "an industry full of smoke and mirrors", onboarding answers).
- **Circumstances:** comparing several vendors in open tabs, mid-project, paying out of a tight budget, wanting it shipped this week.
- **The desire:** "A supplier I can check, not one I have to trust." Lot on the label matches the COA, it ships today, someone answers email.
- **What keeps them lying awake:** a lot that doesn't match its paperwork, which means the work done with it can't be relied on.
- **Objections:** "Is the COA real and is it for my lot?" · "Will it actually ship today?" · "No returns once shipped, so what if it's wrong?"
- **Their words (working, to validate):** "Show me the lot number." · "Who ran the test?" · "Is this COA even current?" · "Did it ship yet?"
- **Current situation → desire:** from trusting screenshots to checking a lot-matched COA before paying.
- **Entry point:** single vials (Tesamorelin 10mg, MOTS-C 40mg, CJC 1295 No DAC / Ipamorelin blend) and the COA library.

## 4. Voice
**Sender persona (proposed 2026-11-19, pending approval):** **Claire from Meta Molecule**, speaking for the lab-supply team ("we", "our team"). Plain, precise, easy to say; reads like a person at a supplier who answers email, which is the brand's whole point. She never claims personal results, expertise or use. Founder notes stay from the founder (name to confirm).

How the brand talks (onboarding answers: "professional, confident, science-driven and approachable"):
- **Precise:** lot numbers, report IDs, times. "Ordered before 1 p.m. CT? It ships today."
- **Confident, not loud:** states the standard and shows the document. "Every claim here has a document behind it."
- **Science-driven:** explains documentation in plain language (what HPLC measures, identity vs purity).
- **Approachable:** short sentences, invites replies. "Questions about a COA? Reply and a person answers."
- **Words to use:** COA, lot, batch, report, documentation, research catalog, ships today, U.S. fulfillment.
- **Words to avoid:** everything in compliance.md §6 and ruo-peptides.md §3; also hype ("insane", "game-changer") and "pharmaceutical grade".
- **Required disclaimer:** default "FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY. NOT FOR HUMAN OR VETERINARY USE OR CONSUMPTION." until the brand approves its own (body + footer).
