## Ground rules for this month
- **Research-use-only**
  - Every email talks to a research buyer about documentation, lots, availability, shipping and support
  - No bodies, outcomes, use or dosing
  - Products named by compound and size; categories by format (Vials, Blends, Bundles)
  - **Not featured until reviewed:** Reta, Tirz, "GLPs", GLOW, CEO in a Bottle, strips, capsules, Amino H2O
- **Offers are proposals** (SOP §10: none approved yet), capped at the brand's 25%. Approved CTAs only (default: View COA Library, Explore Research Catalog, Review Specifications).
- **Cold sender, new account:** weeks 1–2 go to the most engaged contacts only (FC | Engaged 90), widening to Engaged 120/180 once opens hold above 35% and complaints stay low. Dates move with the compliance clearance.
- **80/20:** 8 value emails (documentation, education, shipping, wholesale) and 4 offer emails (Black Friday weekend + winback).
- **Audiences** use Fluid's master-sheet names, built in Customer.io as data-driven segments once events flow (spec at the bottom). If there's no imported list, the first month narrows to whoever the welcome pop-up collects.
- Every email: approved disclaimer in the body near products and in the footer; physical address in the footer.

## Segments to build in Customer.io (spec for the team)
Depends on which events the WooCommerce integration sends (to confirm: `placed_order`, product viewed, checkout started, added to cart, page/site activity).
- **FC | Engaged 90 / 120 / 180:** opened or clicked any email, OR performed placed_order / checkout_started / added_to_cart / product_viewed / page view, in the last N days, AND email subscribed.
- **FC | Engaged 30 ex. Purchasers (New Subs):** opened/clicked/viewed product in 30 days AND created in last 30 days AND no placed_order in 30 days AND subscribed.
- **FC | Placed Order 7d / 14d / 30d:** placed_order at least once in the last N days.
- **FC | Lapsed Customers (Winback):** placed_order ever AND none in the last 180 days AND subscribed.
