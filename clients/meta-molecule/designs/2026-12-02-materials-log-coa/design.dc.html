Most materials logs have a "COA ref" column. Most of the time it's blank, because the paperwork that came with the order gave you nothing to write in it.
The four checks

What each row needs

✓Lot number01

It matches the label on your vial.
✓Testing lab02

It's named on the report, not just "third-party tested".
✓Method03

It says how purity and identity were tested, for example HPLC and MS.
✓Date04

It shows when the sample was tested.

If one of the four is missing, the row stays blank.
