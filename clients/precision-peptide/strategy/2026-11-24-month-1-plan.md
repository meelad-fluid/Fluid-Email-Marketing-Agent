# The Precision Peptide Company — Month 1 plan (December 2026)
_Prepared 2026-11-24 · 12 emails + 5 SMS · Platform: Customer.io_

**Status: on hold. Compliance gate is ESCALATE.** These are briefs, not copy. Nothing gets written, designed or sent until counsel approves the claim set, disclaimers, CTAs and offers (compliance.md).

## How this month works
- **Fix inbox placement first.** Flows open at 5%, so week 1 goes only to the most engaged people.
- **Lead with trust, not outcomes.** Every send sells legitimacy, format or process. Never a body result.
- **80/20.** Nine value sends, three offer sends.
- **No Black Friday send.** Nothing can clear legal review by Nov 27, and a cold blast would hurt inbox placement.
- **One sender, one domain.** Proposed: Sarah from The Precision Peptide Company, from the precisionpeptidecompany.com domain.

## Audience ramp (Customer.io segments to build)
1. **Week 1:** Engaged 30
2. **Week 2:** Engaged 60
3. **Weeks 3–4:** Engaged 90
4. **Only if opens hold above 25%:** Engaged 120

**Segment specs for Customer.io** (master sheet, adapted)
- **Engaged N:** opened or clicked any email, or performed `order_created`, in the last N days, AND subscribed
- **New Subs (Engaged 30 ex. Purchasers):** created in the last 30 days, opened or clicked, no `order_created` in 30 days
- **Placed Order 7d / 14d / 30d:** performed `order_created` in the last 7 / 14 / 30 days
- **Lapsed Customers (Winback):** `order_created` ever, none in the last 180 days, subscribed
- **Always excluded:** Unsubscribed, Invalid Email Address
