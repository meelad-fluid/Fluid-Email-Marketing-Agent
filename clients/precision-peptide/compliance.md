## 6. Never write (until counsel says otherwise)
- Any weight, appetite, metabolism, muscle, recovery, sleep, energy, focus, skin, hair, gut, aging or longevity outcome
- "FDA approved", "FDA-registered" as proof of product approval, "pharmaceutical grade", "clinically proven", "100% pure"
- That compounded GLP-1 is the same as, or a generic of, any branded drug
- Dosing, titration, injection or "protocol week 4" style timelines
- Testimonials about personal results, before/after
- Category names that promise an effect ("Weight Loss", "Performance", "Longevity") in subject lines or headlines
- Retargeting or winback that infers a condition ("time for your next refill", "keep your progress")
