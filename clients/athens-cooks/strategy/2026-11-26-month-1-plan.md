# Athens Cooks — Month 1 plan (Nov 27 – Dec 26, 2026)
Status: draft · needs approval

**The plan:** 12 emails and 5 SMS that ride Black Friday into Christmas, lead with specific makers and real prices (what worked in November), and push the three doorways the data shows: classes, gift cards and knife sharpening.

**Ground rules**
- **From:** Lucy from Athens Cooks (proposed). Founder notes come from Courtney and Grady
- **Max discount is 10%.** Sale prices are vendor-approved only, "Selected items only. Prices as marked."
- **Default audience:** FC | Engaged 120. The 20,905 full-list send earned the lowest RPR
- **Test:** A/B the sender name (Lucy from Athens Cooks vs Athens Cooks) on email #1
- **Mix:** about 70% value and gift-guide sends, 30% promo (holiday month)
- **Before the first send:** build the segmentation master sheet in Klaviyo (Engaged 90/120/180, New Subs, Placed Order 7d/14d/30d, Lapsed)

**Why these angles**
- Specific maker plus price beat checklists 3–6× on RPR (Nov campaigns)
- Gift cards, classes and sharpening are top first-order products (orders, last 90 days)
- 597 lapsed repeat buyers and 379 VIPs ($358+) haven't been mailed separately
