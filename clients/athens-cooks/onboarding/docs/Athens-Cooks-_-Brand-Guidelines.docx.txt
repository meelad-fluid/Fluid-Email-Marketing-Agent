Describe your brand in 2 sentences

We are a kitchen store for people seeking high quality, ethically sourced and sustainable products for their families. We aim to be a central hub for all things food related, from kitchen tools and utensils, to gourmet organic foods, spices and wine to cooking education education. We have one location in Athens, GA, but we ship nationwide.


What about your service/product is unique?

See above. Basically, we we have not competition locally. Nationally, service and responsiveness is a factor, but the area we want to focus on is our selection of product. Everything is carefully curated to fit with our values of sustainability, ethical sourcing and quality. We believe in everything we sell, and don't carry anything we would not want our families to use.

What does your ideal customer look like?

Our customers tend to be middle aged, looking to upgrade or add to their kitchen wares. Probably 65% Female. They are in to cooking, often as a hobby, and appreciate having good tools, good design and beauty. They actively look for alternatives to Amazon, hate Walmart and like to support small business. They are conscious of dangerous chemicals, microplastics and environmental issues. They want to feel safe, protect and nurture their family, appreciate quality, want to be surrounded by beauty and reject the disposability of society. They reject consumerism in theory, but justify their consumerism because they are buying quality and fairly sourced products. They are knowledgeable, but need someone like us to help them curate products because it can all be so overwhelming. If they don't have a store like ours in their town, they are forced to find someone like us, and that is a time consuming process. They want to live their morals, but it is hard to do so because few businesses make it easy for them.
