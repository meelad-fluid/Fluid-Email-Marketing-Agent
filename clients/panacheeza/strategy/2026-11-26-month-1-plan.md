# Panacheeza — Month 1 Plan (December 2026)
_Prepared 2026-11-26 · updated 2026-11-27 (calendar format) · Status: draft, needs client approval_

**The plan in one line:** sell the refill and the gift in December, using the two things that earned most this fall: quick uses and a free-bag style offer.

**Ground rules**
- 12 emails + 5 SMS · Dec 1–29 · BFCM through Cyber Monday (Nov 30) stays as already planned
- 8 value sends · 4 offer sends (80/20 is relaxed for gifting season)
- Max discount 15% · gifts-with-purchase are proposals until approved
- Default audience is FC | Engaged 90 · the full list only for the gift offer launch
- From: Sofia from Panacheeza (proposed) · founder notes from Janessa
- Lead with Original · Truffle and Hot & Spicy as the upsell (client brief)

**Segments to build first** (Fluid master sheet, none exist yet)
- FC | Engaged 90 · FC | Engaged 120 · FC | Engaged 180
- FC | Engaged 30 ex. Purchasers (New Subs)
- FC | Placed Order 7d · FC | Placed Order 14d · FC | Placed Order 30d
- FC | Lapsed Customers (Winback)
