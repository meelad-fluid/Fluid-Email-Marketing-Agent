Describe your brand in 2 sentences

Panacheeza was born out of personal neccessity in our home kitchen. There was a distinct gap in the market for a clean-label dairy free parmesan that offered the same flavor forward nostalgic taste of traditional parmesan without preservatives, additives, and that is shelf stable. We redefined the category. Our mission is to continue to serve the community with a flavorful clean-label dairy free alternative for those who are dairy intolerant and/or seeking a healthier alternative to parmesan.

What does your ideal customer look like?

35-60 year old female, plant-based, vegan, or flexitarian. The main pain point they face is flavor first and clean label second. Most of them have tried everything under the sun and land on Panacheeza due to its flavor and label. They want to have to feel like they are having the same parmesan experience as traditional parmesan but aligned with their health and lifestyle choices. Common road blocks have been flavor and chemical enriched alternatives. Panacheeza is the exact opposite in every category.


What struggles/challenges/pain-points your customer has which can be solved by your product?

Flavor and ingredients are the main pain points while providing an alternative to those who are sensitive to dairy and/or choose to live a lifestyle away from dairy.
