## 2. The brand

**What they sell** (site, Nov 2026)

- **Bottoms and shoes:** pants, shorts, Alberto jeans, Magnanni Italian shoes ($450)

- **Also:** gift cards, wholesale for pro shops and retailers

**Founder story** (site /pages/about-us)
- Georg Roth was born in Germany and started in fashion at 17, designing ties and bow ties
- He learned fabric in Milan's markets, Paris showrooms and small-town tailors, not business school
- He came to California and "found home"
- He started the line because American menswear felt stiff and lacked personality

**Why it's different, in one sentence**
- Luxury Pima basics plus a real person who helps you get the fit right: they call every customer after an order (brief)

**Proof bank**
- Rated 4.7/5 on the site, no review count shown (site home)
- Free U.S. shipping over $150 (site)

- Carried by golf pro shops and boutiques (stockists, Haggin Oaks trunk show)
- "No shrinkage, no twisted seams" is claimed by stockists (M Penner, Haggin Oaks). **Not used until the client confirms it.**

**Offers and rules**
- **Max discount: 10%** (brief)
