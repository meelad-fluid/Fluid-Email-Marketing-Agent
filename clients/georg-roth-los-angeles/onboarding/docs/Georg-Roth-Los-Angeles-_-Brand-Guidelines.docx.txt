What does your ideal customer look like?

1. What is the age and gender of your target audience? Our primary target audience is men ages 35–65 who want to look polished, confident, and stylish without overthinking their wardrobe. We also serve women shopping for men, especially wives, girlfriends, and family members looking for high-quality gifts or wardrobe upgrades. 2. What are some common pain points they face? Many of our customers want to dress better but do not want shopping to feel complicated. They often struggle to find clothing that fits well, feels comfortable, looks refined, and works for multiple occasions. They may also be tired of cheap basics, poor fit, uncomfortable fabrics, or clothing that feels either too casual or too formal. 3. What goals do they want to achieve, or how do they want to feel, look, or be? They want to look confident, successful, modern, and put together. They want clothing that makes them feel comfortable but still elevated. Our customers want to be able to go from work, travel, dinner, events, or weekends without needing to change their entire look. 4. What are some common roadblocks preventing them from achieving these goals? The biggest roadblocks are time, confusion, and lack of guidance. Many men do not enjoy shopping or do not know what pieces work best together. They may also have trouble finding brands that offer both comfort and sophistication, or they may not know how to build a wardrobe that feels easy, stylish, and complete.
