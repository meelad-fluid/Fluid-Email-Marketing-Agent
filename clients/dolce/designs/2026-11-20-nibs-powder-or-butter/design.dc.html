All three come from the same cacao bean, but they do very different jobs in the kitchen. Here's what each one is for, so you grab the right bag before Thanksgiving.

One bean, three very different jobs

01
Cacao Nibs
These are the crunch.

They're crushed pieces of the cacao bean, and they taste like dark chocolate without any sugar. Fold them into cookie dough or scatter them over brownies before they bake.

Shop Cacao Nibs
02
Cacao Powder
This is the chocolate flavor.

It's the bean with most of the fat pressed out and ground fine. Use it anywhere a recipe calls for cocoa, like brownies, chocolate cake or a mug of hot cocoa.

Shop Cacao Powder
03
Cacao Butter
This is the melt.

It's the pure fat pressed from the bean, and it's what gives chocolate its snap. Melt it down when you want to make your own chocolate at home.

Shop Cacao Butter

Homemade chocolate bark, the easy holiday gift

1Melt ½ cup Cacao Butter over low heat.
2Whisk in ½ cup Cacao Powder and 3 tbsp maple syrup until it's smooth.
3Spread it thin on parchment, scatter Cacao Nibs on top and chill it for 30 minutes.
