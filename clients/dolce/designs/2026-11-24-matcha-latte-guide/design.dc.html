Three recipes for your matcha

The café matcha latte, made in your own kitchen

You already have the matcha, so you're most of the way there. Here are three lattes our team makes on repeat, and each one takes about two minutes.

Stock up on matcha
01 · Hot

The coconut matcha latte

Creamy without any dairy, and it's the one we make most mornings.

1Whisk 1 tsp Ceremonial Matcha into 2 oz of hot water (not boiling) until it's frothy.
2Stir 2 tbsp Coconut Milk Powder into 6 oz of hot water.
3Pour the coconut milk over the matcha and sweeten it if you like.
Add Coconut Milk Powder
02 · Iced

The strawberry matcha latte

It's the pink and green one from the café menu, and it costs a lot less to make at home.

1Stir 1 tbsp Freeze-Dried Strawberry Powder into 6 oz of cold milk and pour it over ice.
2Whisk 1 tsp Ceremonial Matcha into 2 oz of water.
3Pour the matcha slowly over the top so the layers stay separate.

03 · Simple

Just matcha and water

This is the traditional way to drink it. It's smooth enough that it doesn't need milk, which is why we use our Ceremonial Reserve for it.

1Sift 1 tsp Ceremonial Reserve into a bowl.
2Add 3 oz of water at about 175°F.
3Whisk it quickly in a zigzag until a fine foam forms on top.
Try Ceremonial Reserve

Three small things that make a big difference

Keep the water just off the boil, because boiling water makes matcha taste bitter.

Sift it first so you don't end up with little clumps at the bottom of the cup.

Store your bag sealed in a cool, dark spot so it stays bright green.

A pound makes a lot of lattes, but they go faster than you'd think once you start making them at home.
