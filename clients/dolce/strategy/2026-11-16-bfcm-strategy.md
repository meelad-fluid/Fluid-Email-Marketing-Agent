# Dolce Superfoods — campaign strategy, Nov 18 → Dec 2, 2026
_Built 2026-11-16 from Klaviyo campaign results (last 60 days) and Klaviyo/Shopify order data (14,762 paid orders, 365 days). Supersedes 2026-11-16-first-campaigns.md. Offers are proposals until Jeff approves them. Sender: Hannah from Dolce (proposed) unless marked as Jeff._

## What the data says
1. **Matcha and cacao run the business, not instant coffee.** Last 90 days: Ceremonial Matcha **$204k (23% of $882k)**, Ceremonial Reserve $48k; the cacao family (nibs $57k, powder $35k + $18k, butter $33k) **≈$143k**. Instant coffee $21k (11th). The brief's "#1 is instant coffee" is out of date.
2. **Cacao is the front door.** Top products in first orders: Ceremonial Matcha (172), Cacao Powder (167 + 148 for the second listing), Blue Spirulina (154), Ceylon Cinnamon (145), Cacao Butter (136), Cacao Nibs (129).
3. **This is a replenishment brand.** 26% of first-time buyers order again, **72% of them reorder the same product**, and the median gap is **55 days** (25–92). That gives the two most valuable audiences: **one-time buyers 30–60 days out (572)** and **customers due to reorder (441)**.
4. **Big baskets are normal.** Average order $213, median $106; **31% of orders are already $160+**. A spend-based offer pushes the median order up instead of discounting small ones.
5. **Engaged beats wide.** Guides sent to the 90-day engaged list earn $0.69–0.94 per recipient. The free-matcha sends to the 180d/365d/full lists earned $0.04–0.13. Wide lists go only on Black Friday and Cyber Monday, and only to proven buyers.
6. **Room to cross-sell.** 2,283 cacao buyers have never bought matcha; 1,139 matcha buyers have never bought coffee or mushrooms.
7. **What's proven with codes:** DOLCE15 (458 orders, $71k in 90 days) and "mystery" (238 orders, $50k).

**Shape of the plan:** 9 emails in 15 days (the peak, then back to ~3 a week). Value guides frame the sale: cacao for holiday baking, matcha cost per cup, the family story. The Black Friday offer is spend-tiered to lift the median order. Early access goes to VIPs. The reorder push comes right after Cyber Monday, when the 55-day gap lines up for October buyers.
