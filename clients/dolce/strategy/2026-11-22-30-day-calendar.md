# Dolce Superfoods: 30-day campaign calendar, Mon Nov 23 → Tue Dec 22, 2026
Status: needs approval · first send Mon Nov 23, 2026

_Built 2026-11-22 on top of the BFCM plan (Nov 16), the Klaviyo results of the last 60 days and the 365-day order data. Offers are proposals until Jeff approves them. Sender: Hannah from Dolce (proposed) unless marked Jeff._

**The plan in one line:** a full Black Friday arc with an email every day of the sale, then a December built on what already earns for Dolce (guides, reviews, co-packing) and a hard Christmas shipping deadline.

## What the data says
- **Guides to the engaged list are the consumer engine.** The "which one is right for you" guides earn $0.69–0.94 per recipient on the 90-day engaged list.
- **Reviews land.** "In their words, in capital letters" was the top campaign Nov 10–16: $3,766 from 14 orders.
- **Co-packing is the best earner per send.** "Most co-packers won't talk to you under 10,000 units" made $1,861 from 6 orders to 1,372 people ($1.36 per recipient). The co-pack send in the audit hit $3.06 per recipient.
- **Wide lists don't pay.** The free-matcha sends to 11–15k people earned $0.04–0.24 per recipient. The Nov 20 gift guide to 11.2k made $593 ($0.05). December stays on engaged lists.
- **Dolce is a restock brand.** 72% of repeat buyers reorder the same product, about 55 days later. The Replenishment flow was the top flow last week ($2,169).
- **Last week (Nov 16–22):** email $17,298 from 89 orders across 7 sends; campaigns $10,127, flows $7,171.

## The month's angles
1. **Black Friday Stock-Up** (retention): 8 emails, Nov 25 → Dec 1. One email every day of the sale, and the close earns most.
2. **Which One Is Right** (retention + acquisition): 4 emails. Guides and recipes, the account's best consumer content.
3. **Proof From Real Customers** (acquisition): 2 emails. Reviews and the price objection, aimed at engaged people who have never bought.
4. **Back for Your Usual** (lapsed + restock): 2 emails. Restock after the sale and the matcha win-back.
5. **Launch in 2027** (B2B track): 1 email to wholesale and co-packing leads.

Plus 2 founder notes from Jeff and the Christmas shipping deadline.
