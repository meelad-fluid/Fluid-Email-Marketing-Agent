# Dolce Superfoods — first campaigns
_Proposed 2026-11-16 from the Onboarding Doc. Offers are proposals until Jeff approves them._

## 1

```
Email Campaign name: FREEMATCHA last call
Sent to: FC | 90-day engaged email list + FC | Black Friday Early Access
Discount (if applicable): Existing offer, no new discount — free 1 lb Culinary Matcha ($23.99 value) on orders $160+, code FREEMATCHA, one-time purchases only, ends Nov 19
Angle: Most people on this list already buy coffee, cacao or matcha every month. $160 is a normal stock-up for them, not a stretch, so the free pound is a bonus on a basket they'd buy anyway. Show the basket, not the freebie.
Goals: Engaged buyers see three ready-made $160 baskets built from the bestsellers (e.g. instant coffee 3 lb + cacao + ceremonial matcha) and realise they're one stock-up away from the free bag. One action: build your basket. Designed: hero = "Free matcha ends tomorrow" + the code; three basket cards with totals; how to redeem in 3 steps; deadline.
Excluding: FC | BFCM 2026 free-matcha buyers ($160+ order Nov 15–19); purchasers in the last 7 days; FC | Shopify Tags contains wholesale; HIGH VALUE - CO-PACKING CLIENTS; FC | Sunset - Unengaged 90d
```
**From:** Hannah from Dolce (proposed persona)

**Why now:** the FREEMATCHA sends went to the 180-day / 365-day / full lists and earned $0.04–0.13 per recipient at 19–32% opens. The 90-day engaged list earned $0.69–0.94 on its best sends. The offer is fine; the audience and the framing (the freebie instead of the stock-up) were the issue. Send Nov 18, with an SMS on the 19th.

## 2

```
Email Campaign name: The cost of a cup
Sent to: FC | 90-day engaged email list
Discount (if applicable): None
Angle: Jeff was a CPA, so he does the math: what a cup of organic instant coffee costs when you buy the 3 lb bag versus a café latte or a boutique jar. Cheap isn't the catch; buying in bulk with their own facilities is how they keep organic quality at that price.
Goals: Daily coffee drinkers who've been paying boutique prices see the per-cup number and why it's that low (bulk buying, sourcing relationships, certified organic, SQF facility) and stock up before the holidays. One action: shop the Organic Instant Coffee. Plain text from Jeff, one link, the per-cup figure in the second sentence. Follow up with a designed resend to non-openers 3 days later (Nov 3's resend earned $1.77 per recipient).
Excluding: Purchasers in the last 7 days; anyone who bought Organic Instant Coffee in the last 30 days; FC | Shopify Tags contains wholesale; FC | Sunset - Unengaged 90d
```
**From:** Jeff Weaver, Dolce Superfoods (founder note); the designed resend from Hannah from Dolce

**Why now:** a value send between the free-matcha push and Black Friday, built on the #1 seller (brief) and the brand's core promise (premium without the premium price). It answers the top objection ("How come it costs so little?") with the founder's own credibility. Guides and "which one" sends to this list are the consumer engine ($0.69–0.94). Needs from client: the per-cup figure confirmed (servings per 3 lb bag).

## 3

```
Email Campaign name: Launch in Q1, start your run now
Sent to: FC | Shopify Tags contains wholesale + FC | Joined Wholesale Lead
Discount (if applicable): None
Angle: A brand that wants product on shelves by spring has to start formulation and packaging now. Dolce takes small runs other co-packers won't, from 100 units, so a first run doesn't need a big minimum.
Goals: Wholesale customers and leads with their own brand idea see the path from quote to first run (formulation, packaging options, first-run volumes from 100 units) and request a quote before the holidays. One action: get a quote. Designed, B2B tone: three-step "idea → formula → your label" graphic, packaging options row (pouches, jars and tins, stick packs, pods), one proof point (SQF/GMP facility).
Excluding: HIGH VALUE - CO-PACKING CLIENTS; IS | Co-packing Leads (already in the co-pack flow); anyone who submitted the co-packing form in the last 60 days; IS | Wholesale Leads (Do not use this list for marketing emails)
```
**From:** Hannah from Dolce (proposed persona)

**Why now:** B2B is the highest-value audience per email. "Your label. Our floor. From 100 units." earned $3.06 per recipient, the best of the period, and its follow-up earned $1.36. There's been no B2B send since Oct 2. It also keeps B2B buyers out of the consumer Black Friday noise. Needs from client: current lead times and whether the 100-unit minimum still stands.
