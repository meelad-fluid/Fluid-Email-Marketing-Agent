## SECTION 2 · The recipe that uses all three
HEADING: The gift you can make tonight with all three
STEP 1: Melt ½ cup Cacao Butter over low heat.
STEP 2: Whisk in ½ cup Cacao Powder and 3 tbsp maple syrup until it's smooth.
STEP 3: Spread it thin on parchment, scatter Cacao Nibs on top and chill it for 30 minutes.
LINE: Break it up, put it in a jar with a ribbon, and nobody will guess it took you ten minutes.
