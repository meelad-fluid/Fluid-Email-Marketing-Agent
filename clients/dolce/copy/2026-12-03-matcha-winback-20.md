- **Avatar point:** situation (they used to make matcha at home, ran out, and the reorder slipped) to desire (their usual morning cup back). Win-back tone is a friendly check-in with an offer, not a hard sell.
- **Framework:** BAB. Before = the empty tin (hero subheadline). After = their matcha back (product row). Bridge = the code with a deadline (close).
- **Why Dec 3:** a 20% no-minimum matcha code before or during Black Friday would undercut the proposed BF tiers (15% $100+ / 20% $200+) and the FREEMATCHA push. Right after Cyber Monday it's a clean ask, and it doesn't overlap the Dec 2 "Time to restock?" email (that one goes to people 55–110 days out; this one is 120+ days).
- **Why 120 days:** the median gap to a 2nd order is 55 days (most fall within 25–92), so 120+ days without an order means they've genuinely lapsed rather than being slightly late.
- **Subject pick:** "We saved you 20% off your matcha" is personal and specific, and it keeps SALE/OFF out of caps.
- **Product blocks:** name and button only (no prices, so nothing goes stale). Cart links use the same variant IDs as the tested FREEMATCHA links; retest with MATCHA20 once the code exists.
