# El Viejon — Month 1 Plan (Nov 25 – Dec 22, 2026)
_Prepared Mon Nov 23, 2026 · Status: draft, needs client approval_

**The plan in one line:** close BFCM strong, sell Christmas early because every bag is made to order, then go back to founder-led value sends with fewer, stronger emails.

**The month's angles**
1. **Black Friday → Cyber Monday:** the last big sale of the year (Nov 27–30)
2. **Made to order, so order early:** the Christmas deadline (Nov 30 – Dec 4)
3. **Abuelo's table:** heritage, gifting and the holiday spread (Dec 4–15)
4. **Keep the bag full:** reorder and winback (Dec 18–22)

**Ground rules for the month**
- **From:** Rosa from El Viejon (proposed) · founder notes from Pedro
- **Default audience:** FC | 90-day engaged (exists in Klaviyo today)
- **Go wider only for BF and CM:** FC | Engaged 120
- **Cadence:** 12 emails + 5 SMS in four weeks, down from 3.5 a week, because opens fell to 25% on Nov 23
- **Offers:** 20% for BFCM is a proposal (the form says 15% max) · everything after Cyber Monday stays at 15% or less
- **Segments to build** (Klaviyo is read-only for us until an admin allows drafts)
  - FC | Engaged 120
  - FC | Placed Order 7d · 14d · 30d
  - FC | Lapsed Customers (Winback)
  - FC | Engaged 30 ex. Purchasers (New Subs)
  - Two extra cohorts: one-time buyers 25–60 days · VIP spend $180+

**Christmas timing (to confirm with the client):** 7–14 business days to make + 3–5 to ship = 10–19 business days. For the best chance of arriving by Dec 24, the order date is about Nov 30 – Dec 2. The plan treats Wed Dec 2 as the last call.

## Email campaigns
