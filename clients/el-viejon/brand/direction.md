# El Viejon — creative direction (running summary)
_Updated 2026-11-23 from the onboarding answers, website, packaging and live Klaviyo campaigns. No past design files viewed yet (Figma is private)._

- **Look:** black pouch on Ranch Gold, chiles and limes as props, the round cowboy badge. Client wants it high end, Apple-level: lots of space, one hero per frame.
- **Type:** western slab display for short words, condensed caps for labels, clean sans for body.
- **Tone:** grandfather legacy, Mexican pride, hard work, direct and unapologetic. The list is "Familia".
- **What's worked:** founder plain-text notes ("Palabras Del Viejon"), short punchy subjects ("Your mouth wants salt. Give it salt."), VIP-only early access.
- **Offers in use:** 10% welcome (VIEJON10), 20% BFCM (FAMILIA20, above the form's 15% max), free shipping over $75.
- **Avoid:** sombrero/maraca clichés, organic or health claims, mass-market feel.
