# El Viejon — Onboarding Doc
_Prepared Mon Nov 23, 2026 · Sources: onboarding answers, elviejonbeefjerky.com (home, FAQ, Our Story, products), pouch label, Klaviyo (last 60 days), Klaviyo Shopify order events (last 90 days / 365 days), one shop.app listing_

**The short version:** a large, healthy account (email + SMS drove 42% of store revenue in the last 60 days) where the founder voice is the strongest value lever and the post-purchase, cart and winback flows are leaving money on the table.

**Campaigns, last 60 days**
- **Best by revenue per recipient**
  - "Your 20% off is live!" · VIP early access · 20% · $4.81 RPR (970 recipients)
  - "20% off, 3 days left" · VIP reminder · 20% · $2.25 RPR
  - "Your VIP offer closes tonight" · VIP last call · 20% · $1.40 RPR
  - "That jerky stash won't refill itself" · BFCM reveal to 90-day engaged · 20% · $0.47 RPR · $17,018
  - "The part I did not plan for" · Palabras Del Viejon #4 founder note · no offer · $0.38 RPR · $12,737
- **What the best share**
  - A small, warm VIP audience
  - Or the founder speaking plainly with no discount (Palabras #2, #4, #5, #7 all 48%–56% opens)
- **Worst by revenue per recipient**
  - "Familia, get your jerky for 20% less" · VIP list recruitment · sent to 50k and 44.7k · $0.06 / $0.03 RPR
  - "Pre sale ends tomorrow" · Nov 23 · 25.4% open · $0.06 RPR
- **What the worst share**
  - Sent beyond the 90-day engaged segment (the 44k–50k lists opened 27%–35%)
  - Discount-heavy subject lines late in a long promo run

**Takeaways for the plan**
1. Keep the founder series ("Palabras Del Viejon") as the value backbone: it's the best non-sale revenue.
2. Keep campaigns on the 90-day engaged segment; go wider only for Black Friday and Cyber Monday.
3. Today's 25% open after 11 days of BFCM sends says the list is tired: fewer, stronger sends through December.
4. VIPs pay back 10x per recipient: grow the VIP list (3,412 customers qualify by spend, 1,100 are on the list).
5. Fix the money flows first: post-purchase, cart and winback.

## 4. Voice

**Sender persona (proposed):** Rosa from El Viejon
- Warm, easy to say, fits a family-run Mexican-American brand
- Sends campaigns and flows; she speaks for the team ("we", "our family")
- Founder notes stay from Pedro (Palabras Del Viejon, post-purchase plain-text emails)
- First month: A/B test "Rosa from El Viejon" against "El Viejon" on one campaign

**How the brand talks**
- **Grandfather legacy:** "His recipes, carefully preserved, serve as the heart of El Viejon." (site)
- **Hard work:** the jerky is the reward after the work is done (site story)
- **Direct and unapologetic:** "Your mouth wants salt. Give it salt." (Klaviyo subject)
- **Familia:** the list is family ("You have been writing back, Familia")
- **Disruptive:** proud to be different from mass-produced jerky

**Words they use:** Familia · carne seca · ranch style · small batch · by hand · Viejon · made to order

**Words to avoid**
- Organic, keto, healthy, gluten-free (not claimed)
- Processed, snack stick, cheap
- Cartoon clichés (sombreros, maracas)

**Required lines:** contains soy on product detail where relevant · made-to-order timing on any deadline email
