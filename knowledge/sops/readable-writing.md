# Writing people can read (every doc and every Slack message)

From Meelad (2026-11-19): our docs and messages read like dense paragraphs. Anything with several items has to be laid out as a list someone can scan.

## Rules
- **One idea per bullet.** If a bullet needs a semicolon, a "First… Second…" or more than one set of brackets, split it.
- **Three or more items → a list, never a comma run.** Products, prices, blockers, open questions, steps, sources: each gets its own line.
- **Group with indented sub-bullets.** A short bold label on the parent line, the items underneath:
  - **Vials**
    - Tesamorelin 10mg · $60
    - MOTS-C 40mg · $119
  - **Bundles** ($70.68–$480, about 7% off list)
    - The Library Bundle · $480
- **Sequences are numbered** (steps, "four things legal needs to clear").
- **Lead with the point** in one short line, then the list. No warm-up sentence.
- **Keep sources out of the sentence.** Put them at the end of the bullet in a short tag, e.g. "(site /shop)", or in one "Sources" line at the end of the section. Never stack two brackets in a row.
- **Short bullets:** aim for under ~20 words. A bullet that runs past two lines is two bullets.
- **Tables** only for real side-by-side comparisons (e.g. brief vs site conflicts), and keep each cell short.
