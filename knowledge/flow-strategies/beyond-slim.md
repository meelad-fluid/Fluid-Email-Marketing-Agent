Post-Purchase Flow (4 Emails)
Email #1: Thank You + Order Confirmation
	•	Send Time: 5 minutes later
	•	Email Topic: Thank you for your order
	•	Design: Plain text
	•	Sending to: New Customers
	•	Goals:
	•	Coming from the founder
	•	Thank them for trusting us
	•	Build a personal connection
	•	Offer support
Email #2: How to use the product OR upsell
	•	Send Time: 1 day later
	•	Email Topic: How to get the best results + A special gift
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: New Customers
	•	Goals:
	•	Explain how to use the products effectively for maximum absorption.
	•	Give a 20% off sale code for their second purchase to encourage a subscription or repeat buy.
	•	Mention Beyond Perks: when they subscribe, they'll earn 1 point per $1 spent and unlock ongoing rewards.
Email #3: Plain text email from the founder
	•	Send Time: 7 days later
	•	Email Topic: Checking in on your journey
	•	Design: Plain text
	•	Sending to: New Customers
	•	Goals: Ask how they are feeling so far. Remind them that true transformation takes consistency.
Email #4: Upsell Offer
	•	Send Time: 2 days later
	•	Email Topic: Ready for the next step?
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: New Customers
	•	Goals:
	•	Suggest a complementary product (e.g., if they bought ZipSlim, suggest ZipBURN or RSN).
	•	When recommending RSN, highlight the clinical glutathione research and its benefits for skin health, cellular defense, and beauty from within.
	•	Mention our collaboration with The Gut Gummy for optimal digestion.
	•	Give a 20% off sale code for their second purchase.
	•	Highlight Beyond Perks subscription benefits and how they can start earning points immediately.


Winback Flow (2 emails)
Email #1: We miss you
	•	Send time: After however many days the average lifecycle of a customer is for this brand
	•	Email topic: It's been a while... Come back and save!
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: First time customers who have never purchased again
	•	Goals: Acknowledge it's been a while. Remind them of how good they felt using the products. Provide a 20% off winback code to encourage a return. Mention Beyond Perks rewards if they subscribe.
Email #2: Come back offer
	•	Send time: After however many days the average lifecycle of a customer is for this brand + 2 days
	•	Email topic: Your 20% off is waiting for you
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: First time customers who have never purchased again
	•	Goals: Create urgency around the 20% off winback code. Reiterate the brand's commitment to helping them stay Fitter, Healthier, and Happier. Introduce a bundle opportunity with The Gut Gummy to reactivate their interest and increase AOV. Emphasize Beyond Perks subscription rewards as an added incentive to return.
