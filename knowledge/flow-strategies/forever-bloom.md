Post-Purchase Flow (4 Emails)

Email #1: Thank You + Order Confirmation
Send Time: 5 minutes after purchase

Email Topic: Thank you for your order - welcome to the family

Design: Plain text (personal, coming directly from the founder)

Sending To: New customers (first purchase)

Goals:

	•	A warm, personal thank-you note written as if coming directly from the founder
	•	Thank them for trusting Forever Bloom with their skin. Acknowledge that choosing a new beauty product is a meaningful decision
	•	Build a personal connection by sharing a brief, genuine message about what this purchase means to the brand
	•	Set expectations: their order ships within 24 hours and they'll receive a tracking notification shortly
	•	Offer personal support. Invite them to reach out with any questions about shade selection, application, or their routine
	•	Make them feel like they've joined a community, not just made a transaction
	•	Soft CTA: "Reply to this email anytime - we're here for you"


Email #2: How to Get the Most Out of Your Foundation + Upsell
Send Time: 1 day after Email #1

Email Topic: Your guide to flawless, skin-first coverage - plus a gift for you

Design: Yes (Educational design with step-by-step visuals and product imagery)

Sending To: New customers

Goals:

	•	Educate the customer on how to use their Forever Bloom product for the best results. Application tips, layering techniques, and how Biomimic® technology adapts to their skin
	•	Build confidence that they made the right choice. Reinforce the key benefits of the product they purchased
	•	Introduce complementary products as a natural upsell. If they purchased a foundation, suggest the Biomimic® Lifting Serum or a Bloom Cream to complete their routine
	•	Offer an exclusive 25% off discount for their second purchase as a loyalty reward
	•	Code: BLOOM2ND
	•	Value: 25% off their next order
	•	Warm, educational tone. This feels like advice from a knowledgeable friend, not a sales pitch
	•	CTA: "Complete your routine - 25% off your next order"

Winback Flow (2 Emails)

Email #1: We Miss You
Send Time: 90 days after last purchase (reflecting the typical repurchase cycle for a premium beauty brand)

Email Topic: We've been thinking about you - come back and bloom

Design: Yes (Warm, nostalgic design with brand imagery)

Sending To: First-time customers who have not made a repeat purchase

Goals:

	•	Re-engage lapsed customers with a warm, genuine message that feels personal, not automated
	•	Acknowledge the time that has passed and express that the brand genuinely misses them
	•	Remind them of what they loved about Forever Bloom - the natural finish, the skin-adaptive technology, the effortless confidence
	•	Highlight any new products or improvements since their last purchase to give them a fresh reason to return
	•	Offer an exclusive 25% off discount as a "welcome back" gesture
	•	Code: COMEBACK25
	•	Value: 25% off their next order
	•	Tone: Warm, empowering, and skin-positive. Make them feel valued and remembered
	•	CTA: "Come back and bloom - 25% off is waiting for you"


Email #2: Come Back Offer - Final Push
Send Time: 7 days after Email #1

Email Topic: Your 25% off is about to disappear - don't miss it

Design: Yes (Urgency-focused design with strong CTA)

Sending To: First-time customers who have not made a repeat purchase and did not convert from Email #1

Goals:

	•	Final reminder that their exclusive 25% off winback offer is expiring soon
	•	Create a sense of urgency. This offer is not permanent, and it's their last chance to use it
	•	Reinforce the key reasons to return: Biomimic® technology, clean formulas, 600,000+ happy customers, and the skin-first philosophy they already believe in
	•	Remind them of the specific products they purchased previously and suggest complementary products to complete their routine
	•	Code: COMEBACK25
	•	Value: 25% off their next order
	•	Strong, warm CTA: "Claim your 25% off before it's gone - your skin is ready to bloom again"
