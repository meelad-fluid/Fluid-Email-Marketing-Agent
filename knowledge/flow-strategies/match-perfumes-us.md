Post-Purchase Flow (4 Emails)
Email #1: Thank You + Order Confirmation
	•	Send Time: 5 minutes later
	•	Email Topic: Thank you for your order
	•	Design: Plain text
	•	Sending to: New Customers

Goals:

	•	Coming from the founder.
	•	Thank them for trusting us.
	•	Build a personal connection.
	•	Offer support.
	•	Reassure them that they made a strong first move toward finding their signature match.
	•	Set expectations around shipping.
	•	Invite them to reach out with any questions about their order or fragrance choice.
Email #2: How to use the product OR upsell
	•	Send Time: 1 day later
	•	Email Topic: How to make your match last longer
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: New Customers

Goals:

	•	Educate customers on how to get the best experience from their fragrance.
	•	Share simple usage tips.
	•	Apply to pulse points.
	•	Moisturize skin first.
	•	Avoid rubbing wrists together.
	•	Store the bottle away from heat and direct light.
	•	If they bought Rouge, describe how to wear it for everyday warmth and elegance.
	•	If they bought Cherry, describe how to wear it for evenings, confidence, and memorable entrances.
	•	If they bought Bridal Musk, describe how to wear it for soft, romantic, lasting presence.
	•	Introduce the idea of building a 3-fragrance wardrobe.
	•	Remind them that orders with 3 fragrances qualify for free U.S. standard shipping.
Email #3: Plain text email from the founder
	•	Send Time: 7 days later
	•	Email Topic: Did you find your match?
	•	Design: Plain text
	•	Sending to: New Customers

Goals:

	•	Check in personally after they have had time to try the fragrance.
	•	Ask how they are liking the scent.
	•	Ask whether it matched the mood they were looking for.
	•	Build relationship and trust by inviting replies, questions, and feedback.
	•	Softly tee up a future review request by making the customer feel heard before asking for public feedback.
Email #4: Upsell Offer
	•	Send Time: 2 days later
	•	Email Topic: Ready for your next match?
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: New Customers

Goals:

	•	Encourage a second purchase by recommending a complementary scent from the approved products.
	•	If they bought Rouge, recommend Cherry for bold evenings or Bridal Musk for a softer signature layer.
	•	If they bought Cherry, recommend Rouge for everyday magnetic warmth or Bridal Musk for romantic elegance.
	•	If they bought Bridal Musk, recommend Rouge for confident warmth or Cherry for sweet, bold drama.
	•	Position the upsell around building a fragrance wardrobe for different moods, outfits, and moments.
	•	Mention free U.S. standard shipping with 3 fragrances as an AOV driver.
