Email 2: Personal Note from the Founder
Send Time: 4 hours after sign up

Email Topic: Why I started LGNDRY Fit

Design: Plain text

Sending to: New subscribers

Goal: Build a genuine relationship by sharing the founder's story, emphasizing the focus on discipline, self-improvement, and community. Invite subscribers to be part of the LGNDRY legacy.


Email 3: Customer Reviews
Send Time: 1 day after sign up

Email Topic: See why everyone is living legendary

Design: Yes

Sending to: New subscribers

Goals:

	•	Build trust through authentic customer reviews and testimonials
	•	Remind subscribers about their 10% welcome discount
	•	Use inspirational and reassuring messaging focused on empowerment and confidence
	•	Showcase how the gear transforms customers' lives

Segment-Specific Testimonials:

	•	Accessories: Feature reviews highlighting quality, durability, and how accessories complete the LGNDRY lifestyle


Email 4: Best-Seller Showcase
Send Time: 1 day after sign up

Email Topic: The pieces you need to perform at your best

Design: Yes

Sending to: New subscribers

Segment-Specific Collections:

	•	Women's Activewear: Define Sculpting Collection (Leggings, Bra, Shorts, Jumpsuit), Muse Collection (High Neck Bra, Halter Tank), Essential Legend Collection (Leggings, Biker Shorts)

	•	Accessories: Hats, socks, snapbacks, beanies.

POST-PURCHASE FLOW
Email 1: Thank You + Order Confirmation
Send Time: 5 minutes after purchase

Email Topic: Thank you for your order

Design: Plain text

Sending to: New customers

Goals:

	•	Thank customers for trusting LGNDRY Fit
	•	Build a personal connection from the founder
	•	Emphasize discipline, growth, and becoming the best version of themselves
	•	Offer support and answer any questions


Email 2: Upsell Offer (10% Off Next Purchase)
Send Time: 1 day after purchase

Email Topic: Elevate your next workout

Design: Yes

Sending to: New customers

Discount Code: NEXT10

Discount Value: 10% Off

Goal: Offer a 10% discount on their next purchase to encourage customers to complete their set, try a new collection, or build their LGNDRY wardrobe.

Segment-Specific Upsell Recommendations:

	•	Women's Activewear: If they bought a legging, recommend complementary bra or top. If they bought a bra, recommend leggings or shorts
	•	Men's Activewear: If they bought a tee, recommend shorts. If they bought shorts, recommend tees or accessories
	•	Accessories: If they bought boxing gloves, recommend performance socks or gym bags. If they bought hats, recommend complementary accessories or training gear


Email 3: Founder Check-In
Send Time: 7 days after purchase

Email Topic: Checking in on your journey

Design: Plain text

Sending to: New customers

Goal: Ask how they are liking their gear. Reiterate LGNDRY's commitment to helping them become the best version of themselves. Build long-term relationship and community.


Email 4: Upsell Reminder (10% Off Next Purchase)

Email Topic: Don't forget your 10% off

Design: Yes

Sending to: New customers

Discount Code: NEXT10
