Email #2: How to use the product OR upsell
Send Time: 1 day later

Email Topic: Before you hit the water, read this

Design: Yes

Sending to: New Customers

Goals:

	•	Improve the ownership experience by helping customers feel prepared before their first session.
	•	If they bought a kayak or SUP, provide setup, inflation, storage, transport, and safety tips.
	•	If they bought windsurfing, kiteboarding, foiling, or sailing gear, provide setup checks, compatibility reminders, and progression-minded advice.
	•	If they bought an electric foil, jet board, underwater scooter, or premium watercraft, provide charging, care, storage, saltwater rinse, and safety reminders.
	•	Softly recommend useful accessories such as dry bags, roof racks, beach dollies, leashes, pumps, replacement parts, maintenance products, or safety gear.

Winback Flow (2 emails)
Email #1: We miss you
Send time: 120 days after last purchase for most retail customers, 45 to 60 days for repeat consumable parts customers, and 180 days for high-ticket or commercial customers

Email topic: Still getting on the water?

Design: Plain text

Sending to: First time customers who have never purchased again

Goals:

	•	Re-engage customers in a helpful, community-driven way.
	•	Ask whether they need anything for their current setup, replacement parts, transport help, maintenance gear, or advice on progressing to the next level.
	•	Segment messaging based on what they purchased when possible.
	•	For Chinook Windsurfing Components and other consumables, remind them to check worn parts before the next session.
	•	For kayaks and SUPs, suggest storage, transport, comfort, and safety add-ons.
	•	For premium products, suggest maintenance, accessories, and upgrade paths.
	•	Make the email feel like a check-in from a real water sports shop, not a generic winback campaign.

Send time: 7 days after Email #1 if they have not purchased

Email topic: A little push to get you back on the water

Design: Yes

Sending to: First time customers who have never purchased again

Goals:

	•	Give lapsed customers a clear reason to come back.
	•	Offer a controlled incentive that protects margins while encouraging repeat purchases.
	•	Code: BACKONWATER10
	•	Value: 10% off eligible items for 72 hours.
	•	Remind them that Liquid Surf and Sail can help them buy right the first time, find hard-to-source gear, and choose products that match their sport, skill level, conditions, and budget.
	•	Use best sellers and category-based recommendations to make the next purchase easy.
	•	Include clear exclusions for select high-ticket items, MAP-restricted brands, custom quotes, and commercial packages where needed.
