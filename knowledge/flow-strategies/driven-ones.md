DRIVEN NEVER STOPS.

Post-Purchase Flow (4 Emails)
Email #1: Thank You and Order Confirmation
	•	Send Time: 5 minutes later
	•	Email Topic: Thank you for your order
	•	Design: Plain text
	•	Sending to: New Customers
Goals
	•	Come from the founder and thank the customer for trusting Driven Ones.
	•	Build a personal connection around the brand’s mission of quality, transparency, education, fair pricing, and service. Reinforce that the larger mission is helping people stay committed to becoming better.
	•	Confirm that the order is being prepared and remind the customer that orders ship within 48 hours.
	•	Offer support through the customer service email and invite questions about order status, documentation, or the COA Library.
	•	Restate that products are for qualified research use only and are not for human or veterinary use.
	•	Avoid adding a promotional message immediately after purchase so the first communication feels personal and service-led.
Email #2: Product Documentation and Second-Purchase Offer
	•	Send Time: 1 day later
	•	Email Topic: Your next step: review the documentation and build your research setup
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: New Customers
	•	Code: SECOND10
	•	Value: 10% off the second purchase
Goals
	•	Provide a research-safe orientation to the purchased product.
	•	Show where to find the product page, COA, FAQs, storage or handling information when officially supplied, and support contact details.
	•	Do not provide human-use dosing, administration, treatment, or protocol instructions.
	•	Suggest complementary catalog items or supplies only as research setup options, with clear product links and factual descriptions.
	•	Offer 10% off the customer’s second purchase with code SECOND10.
	•	Use the offer to encourage a second order while emphasizing responsible documentation review and qualified research use.
Email #3: Plain Text Email from the Founder
	•	Send Time: 7 days later
	•	Email Topic: How is everything going with your order?
	•	Design: Plain text
	•	Sending to: New Customers
Goals
	•	Check in personally and make it easy for the customer to ask questions. Use a genuine founder voice here, not a promotional email disguised as a personal note.
	•	Reinforce that Driven Ones values communication, honesty, and dependable support after the sale.
	•	Ask whether the customer found the product information, COA Library, FAQs, and shipping updates they needed.
	•	Invite feedback about the ordering and fulfillment experience without asking for a product-outcome testimonial.
	•	Remind the customer that all products are sold strictly for research, laboratory, analytical, or educational purposes.
Email #4: Upsell Offer
	•	Send Time: 2 days later
	•	Email Topic: Ready for your next research order?
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: New Customers
	•	Code: SECOND10
	•	Value: 10% off the second purchase
Goals
	•	Encourage a second purchase with 10% off using code SECOND10.
	•	Recommend relevant complementary products or supplies based on the customer’s first purchase, using product-category logic rather than medical or performance claims.
	•	Feature practical additions such as research supplies, COA-linked products, or commonly paired catalog items where appropriate.
	•	Highlight free shipping on orders of $150 or more and the brand’s fast fulfillment promise.
	•	Use a dynamic recommendation block and one primary CTA to shop the recommended collection.
	•	Keep the offer and all product descriptions within the research-use-only policy.
Winback Flow (2 Emails)
Email #1: We Miss You
	•	Send Time: 45 days after the customer’s last purchase, based on the expected replenishment and research-order cycle for this catalog
	•	Email Topic: Still moving your research forward?
	•	Design: Plain text
	•	Sending to: First-time customers who have never purchased again
	•	Code: COMEBACK10
	•	Value: 10% off a returning customer’s order
Goals
	•	Reconnect with customers who have not placed a second order.
	•	Use a personal, motivational tone that reflects “Be a Driven One” and “DRIVEN NEVER STOPS” without implying a health or performance outcome. Connect the return invitation to progress, consistency, and trust in the brand.
	•	Remind the customer about Driven Ones’ core advantages: third-party testing, COAs, fair pricing, dependable support, and fast fulfillment.
	•	Invite them to review new catalog additions, best sellers, supplies, or the COA Library.
	•	Give them 10% off a returning order with code COMEBACK10.
	•	State that the offer is intended to support a future research order and does not change the research-use-only designation.
Email #2: Come Back Offer
	•	Send Time: 7 days after Winback Email #1
	•	Email Topic: Your 10% comeback offer ends soon
	•	Design: Yes
	•	Sending to: First-time customers who have never purchased again
	•	Code: COMEBACK10
	•	Value: 10% off. Expires at the stated deadline.
Goals
	•	Create a final, time-bound reason to return to the catalog without manufacturing scarcity or overstating what the products can do. Make the message bold and direct, but never desperate.
	•	Give the customer 10% off with code COMEBACK10.
	•	Show best sellers, relevant complementary products, and research supplies through a dynamic product block.
	•	Reinforce product documentation, COA availability, customer support, and free shipping on orders of $150 or more.
	•	Use an expiration deadline and a single primary CTA to shop now.
	•	Remind the customer that all products are strictly for research, laboratory, analytical, or educational purposes and are not for human or veterinary use.
